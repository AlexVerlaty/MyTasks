MyTasks.AI

A private Telegram bot that keeps one household’s shopping lists, to-do lists and calendar — stored in the members’ own Google accounts, never in the bot.

What it is

MyTasks.AI is a Telegram bot used by a single family. Members write to it in ordinary Russian — typed or as voice messages — and it edits the shared lists and calendar they already keep in Google. There is no separate app to install and no account to create: the bot is a different way to reach data that already belongs to the people using it.

Access is by invitation from the owner only. There is no public sign-up. Someone who finds the bot and presses Start is placed in a queue for the owner to approve or decline.

What it does

  • Lists. «Buy milk and bread» adds items; «got the milk» ticks them off; «what’s left for the cottage» reads the list back. Lists live in the member’s choice of Google Keep, Google Tasks, Google Sheets or Notion.
  • Calendar. «Put a meeting with mum on Saturday at 2» creates an event; «what’s on today» reads the day across every calendar the person can see.
  • Sharing. One member can grant another access to a list, after a confirmation that names the list, the person and the email Google will send.
  • Voice. Voice messages are transcribed on the owner’s own computer and shown as text before anything is acted on.

What it asks Google for, and why

The bot requests the narrowest scope that can do the job. Where a read-only scope would work, the read-only scope is what it asks for.

ScopeWhy it is needed
calendar.events Creating, rescheduling and cancelling events is the point of the calendar half of the bot. calendar.events.readonly would leave it able to answer «what’s on today» and unable to do anything a person asks for.
calendar.calendars.readonly Reads a calendar’s title and time zone. The time zone is not cosmetic: without it «at 2pm» is written in whatever zone the server happens to run in, and the mistake stays invisible until an appointment is missed.
calendar.calendarlist.readonly Lists which calendars the person is subscribed to, so «what’s on today» can answer from all of them rather than from the primary one alone, and so «put it in the family calendar» can find the calendar by name.
tasks Google Tasks is one of the four places a member may choose to keep their lists. Adding and completing items is the whole feature, so tasks.readonly does not cover it.
drive.file For members who keep lists in Google Sheets. This scope grants access only to files the bot itself created — it cannot see anything else in the person’s Drive. It is used in preference to spreadsheets, which would grant access to every spreadsheet the person owns.

What it never asks for. No access to Gmail, Photos, Contacts, or to Drive files the bot did not create. No scope is requested «in advance» for a feature that does not exist yet.

Where the data goes

Nowhere except Google and Telegram. There is no MyTasks.AI server, no database of user content, and no analytics. The bot runs on the owner’s home computer; credentials are encrypted on that computer’s disk; list and calendar content is read from Google when a message arrives and is not kept afterwards.

The full account is in the privacy policy, including what is stored, for how long, and how to revoke access.

Коротко по-русски

MyTasks.AI — телеграм-бот для одной семьи. Он ведёт общие списки покупок и семейный календарь, которые лежат в ваших собственных Google-аккаунтах, а не у бота. Понимает обычную речь и голосовые. Вход только по приглашению владельца. Бот работает на домашнем компьютере, свои серверы отсутствуют, данные никуда, кроме Google и Telegram, не уходят. Подробности — в политике конфиденциальности.
