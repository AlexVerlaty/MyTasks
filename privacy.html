Privacy policy

MyTasks.AI · last updated 14 August 2026 · back to the homepage

MyTasks.AI is a Telegram bot used by one household by invitation. It has no public sign-up and no business model. This policy describes every piece of data it touches.

Who runs it

The bot is operated by an individual, not a company. Contact: vetryli@gmail.com. The bot itself is @ai_mytasks_bot on Telegram, and it answers only to members the operator has admitted.

What Google data it accesses

Only after the member grants access on Google’s own consent screen, and only within these scopes:

ScopeWhat it is used for
calendar.events Reading the member’s events to answer «what’s on today», and creating, rescheduling, editing or cancelling events they ask for.
calendar.calendars.readonly Reading a calendar’s title and time zone, so times are written in the right zone.
calendar.calendarlist.readonly Listing which calendars the member is subscribed to, so a day can be read across all of them and a calendar can be found by name.
tasks Reading, adding, completing and removing items in the member’s Google Tasks lists.
drive.file Creating and editing the one spreadsheet the bot itself creates for members who keep lists in Google Sheets, and granting a second household member access to it when asked. This scope cannot reach any other file in the member’s Drive.

The bot does not request access to Gmail, Google Photos, Contacts, or Drive files it did not create.

Google Keep

Google Keep has no public API. A member who chooses to keep their lists in Keep supplies the bot with a credential obtained from their own Google session; the bot uses it to read and write only that member’s Keep notes and lists. The credential is encrypted on the operator’s side under a key held outside the file, exactly as the Google OAuth tokens are, and is removed by /unlink.

This access is established outside Google’s OAuth consent screen and is not covered by the scopes above. A member who prefers not to use it can keep their lists in Google Tasks, Google Sheets or Notion instead, all of which use documented APIs.

What it stores, and where

Everything below lives on equipment the operator controls. There is no cloud database and no third-party host.

  • Access tokens for the Google accounts that have been linked, encrypted at rest. These are what allow the bot to act after the member has consented.
  • A roster: for each member, their Telegram user ID, username and display name, the Google address they linked, and their settings — which service holds their lists, which list is the default, whether they receive notifications.
  • An undo journal: for a short period after each change, enough information to reverse it — which list, which item, what it was before. This is what makes the «undo» button work.
  • Logs, in the same place, for diagnosing failures. They record what the bot did and which errors occurred.

List and calendar content is not stored. It is read from Google when a message needs it and discarded once the reply is sent. The bot is a way to reach your data, not a copy of it.

What is sent where

  • To Google — the changes you asked for, and nothing else.
  • To Telegram — the bot’s replies, which necessarily contain the list or calendar content you asked to see. Telegram’s own privacy policy governs messages in transit and at rest on their servers.
  • To Anthropic — to work out what a message means, the text of that message is sent to Claude together with the titles of the member’s lists. The body of a list, the contents of a calendar and any credential are not sent. Voice messages are transcribed locally and never leave that machine as audio.

Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or analytics providers. There are no advertisements, no tracking and no profiling. The bot is not used to train any model.

Limited Use

MyTasks.AI’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the features described on the homepage, is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide those features, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans except with the member’s explicit permission, for security purposes, or as required by law.

How to revoke access

  • In the bot: /unlink for Google Keep, /unlinkcal for Calendar, /unlinktasks for Tasks, /unlinksheets for Sheets, or /logout for all of them at once. The stored credential is deleted from disk.
  • In your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove MyTasks.AI. This works regardless of the bot’s state and takes effect immediately.
  • To be removed entirely, ask the owner to remove you from the household, or write to the address below. Your roster entry and journal rows are deleted.

Revoking access does not delete anything from your Google account: lists and events the bot created remain yours and stay where they are.

Retention

Credentials are kept until revoked. Roster entries are kept while the person is a member. Undo-journal rows and logs are kept only as long as they are useful for reversing a recent change or diagnosing a recent failure, and are not archived.

Children

The bot is not directed at children and is not offered to the public. Membership is granted individually by the owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Because the bot has three users who all know the operator personally, material changes are also told to them directly.
