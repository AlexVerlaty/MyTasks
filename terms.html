MyTasks.AI · last updated 15 August 2026 · back to the homepage

MyTasks.AI is a Telegram bot that keeps shared lists and a calendar for one household. These terms say what it is offered as, and what it is not. They are short because the arrangement is small: there is no company, no fee and no public sign-up.

Who offers it

The bot is run by an individual, not a business. Contact: vetryli@gmail.com. The bot is @ai_mytasks_bot on Telegram.

Who may use it

Membership is by invitation. The operator admits each member individually, and there is no way to join without being admitted. Using the bot means accepting these terms and the privacy policy; if you do not accept them, do not link an account.

Accounts you link are your own. Do not link an account you are not entitled to use, and do not use the bot to reach another person’s data without their knowledge.

What it does with your accounts

Only what you ask it to, within the scopes you granted on Google’s own consent screen. The privacy policy lists every scope and what each one is used for, and it is the authoritative description — these terms do not add to it.

Two things are worth naming here as well, because they reach outside your own account:

  • Sharing a spreadsheet grants another person write access to it and Google emails them. The bot asks for a confirmation naming the address before it does this, and it cannot recall the letter afterwards.
  • Inviting guests to an event makes Google send them an invitation the moment the event is written. The bot shows every address on the confirmation card first, for the same reason.

What it does not promise

The bot runs on a home machine. It is offered as is, with no warranty and no guarantee of availability, correctness or timeliness. It can be offline, it can misunderstand a sentence, and it can be switched off permanently without notice.

Do not rely on it as the only record of anything that matters. Your lists and events live in your Google or Notion account, not in the bot, and they remain there whatever happens to it — that is deliberate, and it is the safeguard this paragraph rests on.

To the extent the law allows, the operator is not liable for any loss arising from use of the bot, including a missed appointment, a deleted item or an unavailable service. Nothing here limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

Natural language and voice

Messages are interpreted by a language model, and voice messages are transcribed automatically. Both can be wrong. Anything that changes or deletes data is shown for confirmation before it happens, and recent changes can be undone from the bot — but the responsibility for checking what you confirmed is yours.

Leaving

  • /unlink, /unlinkcal, /unlinktasks, /unlinksheets or /logout remove the stored credentials.
  • myaccount.google.com/permissions revokes access from Google’s side, whatever state the bot is in.
  • Ask the operator to remove you from the household, and your roster entry goes with it.

The operator may end anyone’s access at any time. Nothing is deleted from your Google or Notion account when access ends: what the bot created stays yours and stays where it is.

Google API compliance

MyTasks.AI’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If these terms change, the date at the top changes with them. Because the bot has a handful of users who all know the operator personally, material changes are also told to them directly.
